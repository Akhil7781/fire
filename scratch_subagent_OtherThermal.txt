I have conducted an exhaustive investigation into independent reference datasets and sources providing ground truth evidence for **Other Thermal Sources** in India for the 2025 study corpus. 

The comprehensive research report has been transmitted to the parent agent via `send_message`. Key findings across all 11 focus domains include:

1. **VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) Product (Gas Flaring)**: Developed by the Earth Observation Group (EOG) at Colorado School of Mines. Uses sub-pixel multi-spectral Planck curve fitting across NIR/SWIR and MWIR/LWIR to extract temperature ($1600\text{--}2200\text{ K}$ for gas flaring vs. $600\text{--}1000\text{ K}$ for biomass), radiant heat, and flared volume (BCM), available in daily and multi-year CSV/KMZ formats.
2. **Global Gas Flaring Tracker (World Bank GGFR)**: Provides site-level point coordinates (`Latitude`, `Longitude`) and annual flared volume estimates (`Flare-Volume-Estimates-by-individual-Flare-Location-2012-2025.xlsx`) under CC BY 4.0, integrating Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20, and NOAA-21 satellite data.
3. **Barren Island Volcanic Activity (GSI & Smithsonian GVP / MIROVA)**: India's only confirmed active volcano ($12.278^\circ\text{N}, 93.858^\circ\text{E}$), with ongoing 2024–2025 eruptive activity. Serves as an absolute ground-truth benchmark for non-industrial volcanic thermal anomalies ($y_i = -1$; FIRMS `type=1`).
4. **Coal Fire Databases (Jharia, Raniganj, Singrauli)**: Delineated by NRSC/ISRO, CMPDI, and BCCL under the 2025–2027 tripartite MoU, supplemented by open Figshare colliery shapefiles (`10.6084/m9.figshare.15181143`) and Global Energy Monitor mine coordinates.
5. **South Asia Brick Kiln Benchmarks (SentinelKilnDB & SustainBench)**: `SentinelKilnDB` (NeurIPS 2025 Datasets & Benchmarks Track, IIT Gandhinagar) provides 62,671 hand-validated brick kilns with Oriented Bounding Boxes (OBBs) across $2.8\text{M km}^2$ in South Asia, enabling precise seasonal (Dec–May) non-industrial identification.
6. **TERI Datasets & Emission Inventories**: National and airshed-level gridded emissions (AEIM-India on ECCAD, NCAP city inventories) providing point-source coordinates and technology-specific emission factors.
7. **Persistent Thermal Anomaly Databases**: NASA FIRMS multi-year static hotspot masks and `type` flag heuristics (0=vegetation, 1=volcano, 2=static land, 3=offshore), combined with object-oriented industrial heat source clustering.
8. **Oil & Gas Infrastructure Maps**: Global Energy Monitor (GGIT & GOIT) and PNGRB providing verified point and polygon geometries for all 23 refineries, 7 LNG terminals, and national pipeline corridors.
9. **Known Continuous Flaring Sites**: Documented facility catalog across Upper Assam (OIL/ONGC), Mumbai High offshore (ONGC), Cambay Basin (Gujarat), Barmer (Cairn Vedanta), KG Basin, and major downstream refineries (Reliance Jamnagar, IOCL Panipat, IOCL Paradip, etc.).
10. **Thermal Power Plant Locations Databases**: Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database (Version 21.0 published Dec 2025, covering 100% of grid-connected generation) and GEM Global Coal/Gas Plant Trackers providing unit-level geocoordinates and operating status.
